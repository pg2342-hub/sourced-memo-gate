What the gate found

With the house rules, Claude admitted every gap, declined every request that only the other client's file could answer, and its figures tie out to the sources. It still does not graduate. A Claude judge keeps finding claims stated more firmly than their sources, such as an expert's "in my experience" written as fact, or "if at least 8 surgeons commit" turned into "only if".

Claude Opus 5.5 at low effort, with the library sent as a cached block: about $0.016 a section, and about $0.016 more for the judge, over 90 recorded sections.
