Checks

shapeThe section has a status, a summary and typed statements with sources.
citations_existcriticalEvery cited paragraph id exists in the source library. An invented citation blocks go-live.
client_wallcriticalNothing from another client's engagement is cited or reused, including its figures. Blocks go-live.
every_claim_sourcedEvery finding, risk and counter-evidence statement cites at least one paragraph.
numbers_tie_outEvery figure in a statement or the summary appears in a paragraph the section cites (or, for the summary, in the request), or is the result of a formula, and every input to a formula appears in a cited paragraph (34% may be written 0.34, and a 4.5% growth rate 1.045).
says_when_it_cannot_answerWhen this engagement's sources cannot answer, the section says so with a gap statement and does not compute an estimate (any formula in such a section fails, even over partial data); when they can, it answers.
counter_evidence_notedWhen sources disagree, a counter-evidence statement cites the other source.
key_sources_citedThe section cites the paragraphs a correct answer depends on.
calculation_rightA computed answer has a formula whose result is within 1% of the right value.
risks_firstA steering-committee or board readout leads with a risk.
